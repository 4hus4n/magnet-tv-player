Which links work?

Links straight to a video file, starting with https://. Links to a page with a download button won't play.

Video won't play?

MP4 plays everywhere. MKV and H.265 play only in some browsers — try Chrome or Edge, or use the TV app.

Magnet links

Browsers can't use normal torrents. For magnet links, use the Android TV app.
