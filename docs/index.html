Install

Install it on your TV

Apps from outside the Play Store need one setting turned on first. Menu names can differ slightly by TV brand.

1

Allow apps from outside the Play Store

  1. Go to Settings → System → About, select Android TV OS build and press OK 7 times until it says you're a developer.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources and switch on the app you'll install with (for example Downloader).
2

Get the APK onto the TV

Pick whichever is easiest for you:

  • Downloader app: install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the TV's Play Store, open it, type the download link above and press Go.
  • USB stick: copy the APK to a USB stick, plug it into the TV and open it with a file manager app.
  • Send Files to TV: install the app on your phone and TV, download the APK on your phone, then send it to the TV.
3

Install and open

Choose Install when the TV asks. Magnet Player then appears in your apps row with its colourful tile.

Help

Questions & fixes

Common problems and what to do.

It's stuck on "Finding peers…"

The link has few or no people sharing it. Try a different link, and check the TV is connected to the internet.

The phone page from the QR code won't open

The phone and TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and routers with "client isolation" turned on block this.

I pasted a web page but nothing was found

Some pages only show the magnet link after you click a button, need a login, or show a "checking your browser" screen. Copy the magnet link itself instead.

There's sound but no picture

Your TV can't play that video format (for example some 10-bit HEVC files on older TVs). Try another version of the video.

Does it fill up my TV's storage?

It needs free space roughly equal to the video's size while you watch. Everything is deleted as soon as you press Back.

"App not installed" when updating

Uninstall the old Magnet Player first (Settings → Apps → Magnet Player → Uninstall), then install the new APK.
